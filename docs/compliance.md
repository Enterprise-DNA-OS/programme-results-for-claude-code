# Reporting controls and their limits

Sources checked 5 November 2026. These are record checks that support a reporting professional's work. They are not a statutory compliance determination. The operator chooses the applicable framework, accounting tier, jurisdiction and reporting commitments. No regulated filing or assurance opinion is supplied.

| Control | Implementation | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Defined measures | Unreviewed aggregation, missing method, unit or definition is flagged | XRB PBE FRS 48 paragraphs 8-9, relevance and verifiability |
| Traceable evidence | Acceptance requires evidence and an observation date; versions retain corrections | XRB PBE FRS 48 paragraphs 8-9, faithful representation and verifiability |
| Complete reporting | Missing, pending or rejected results remain unknown and block closure | XRB PBE FRS 48 paragraphs 8-9, complete and neutral information |
| Comparable periods | Used definitions and accepted calendars cannot be rewritten; results remain separated by period | Treasury summary of comparative information and consistent measurement |
| Target context | Missing agreed targets are flagged | Internal reporting policy supporting contextual comparison, not a prescribed statutory target |
| Weighted rates | Denominators are mandatory and pooled, never average percentages | Internal mathematical integrity control, not a statutory formula |
| Private handling | No sends; original exports and reports stay local with restricted file permissions | Privacy Act principles on storage, accuracy and appropriate use, where personal information is present |
